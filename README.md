 🚀 Project Overview

AI-Powered-Analitics-Demo is a sales performance analytics MVP designed to demonstrate how raw transactional data can be transformed into clear, business-ready insights.

This project focuses on foundational analytics skills: understanding trends, summarizing performance, and communicating results in a way that decision-makers can quickly grasp.

The dashboard was built as a first, minimal version (MVP — *Minimum Viable Product*), similar to what an analyst might deliver in the early stage of an internal analytics or SaaS-style product.

 🎯 Business Problem

Sales teams and managers often have access to large amounts of data, but still struggle to answer basic questions such as:

* How are sales evolving over time?
* What is our overall sales performance?
* Which regions are performing better or worse?

Without clear visuals and structure, these questions slow down decision-making.

 💡 Solution

This project delivers a simple and focused sales dashboard that:

* Summarizes overall sales performance with a headline KPI
* Shows sales trends over time to identify growth or slowdown
* Compares sales by region to highlight performance differences

The goal is not complexity, but clarity and usability.


 📊 Dashboard Features (MVP Scope)

* **Total Sales (KPI Card)**
  A high-level view of overall revenue performance

* **Sales Over Time (Line Chart)**
  Monthly sales trend to identify patterns and changes

* **Sales by Region (Bar Chart)**
  Comparison of sales performance across regions

These three elements form the core of a practical sales performance dashboard.

 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Data Source: Superstore Sales dataset (Kaggle)
* Data Visualization: Power BI
* Data Preparation: Power BI (data type transformation)
* Version Control & Documentation: GitHub
* AI generated Voice: ElevenLabs
* Video Presentation: Loom

 🔍 Key Learnings

* Building an MVP helps focus on the most important business questions
* Proper data typing (e.g., dates) is essential for time-based analysis
* Simple visuals often communicate insights more effectively than complex ones


🔑 Key Insights

Overall sales show a consistent trend over time, highlighting periods of stronger and weaker performance.

Sales performance varies significantly by region, with clear top- and bottom-performing areas.

These patterns indicate opportunities for targeted regional strategies and improved sales planning.
